In April 2024 I became Head of Product & Strategy at PrishaPolicy. In March 2025 I co-founded Vaatun. Eleven months.

People assume the second job is the first job with more risk. That isn't what it felt like.

## What carried over

Understanding insurance broking from the inside. Four years of watching how a brokerage actually runs — the reconciliation, the endorsements, the renewals nobody wants to chase — is the whole reason Vantage exists in the shape it does.

You cannot get this from customer interviews. I tried.

## What didn't carry over

Prioritisation. As Head of Product, prioritisation is choosing between good options with a known budget. As a founder, it's choosing what the company *is*, with the budget as an output rather than an input.

I was much slower at this than I expected to be.

## What I misjudged

That the hard part would be building. The hard part is deciding what not to build while five real customers are asking for it.
