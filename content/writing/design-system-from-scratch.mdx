At Zuddl I built the design system from scratch. There is nothing to show you for it — no Dribbble shot, no case study, no before-and-after. That absence is the most honest thing about the work.

A design system is not a component library. The library is the artefact; the system is the set of agreements that made the library possible. Most of what I did was not drawing.

## The first version is a census, not a design

Before proposing a single token I went through the product and counted. How many greys were in use. How many button heights. How many different ways a modal could close.

The count is the argument. Nobody disputes a number.

## Naming is the hard part

`--color-primary` is a promise you have to keep for years. Every name is a bet about what stays stable and what churns.

The names that survived were the ones describing *role*, not appearance.

## What I'd do differently

Start with the pieces nobody argues about — spacing, radii, type scale. Colour is where opinions live, and leading with it means starting a fight before you've earned the credibility to win it.
